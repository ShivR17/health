# Product

## Register
product

## Users
One person (the owner) tracking a personal fat-loss and health plan toward an engagement (9 Dec 2026) and wedding. Used on a phone, installed as a PWA, several times a day, mostly one-handed: log a meal, check progress, glance at the plan.

## Product Purpose
Personal health plan PWA. Daily food logging with AI nutrition estimates, body measurements, blood panels, InBody scans, trend charts, and meal reminders. Success is a meal logged in under 10 seconds and an honest read of progress toward 72 kg.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, quietly encouraging. A private coach, not a fitness brand.

## Anti-references
Gamified fitness apps (badges, confetti, streak guilt), clinical dashboards, neon dark-mode "biohacker" UIs.

## Design Principles
1. The daily action comes first. Logging a meal is never more than a thumb reach away.
2. Numbers are the content. Make them large, steady, and unambiguous.
3. Sea Glass is the identity. Keep the glass material, hue system, and light/dark themes.
4. Motion confirms, never decorates. Progress should feel earned; nothing should loop.
5. Works offline and on low-end phones. Respect reduced motion and reduced transparency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, 44px touch targets, visible focus, reduced-motion and reduced-transparency support, forced-colors support.
